1.0.0 - 2026-10-14

First stable release of ViBRo Navigator.

  • Offline BRouter routing with route, round trip, and straight-line navigation modes, intermediate stops, and configurable routing profiles.
  • Vibration and optional voice guidance, background navigation with the screen off, and a compass view with surrounding streets from BRouter data.
  • Destination search, voice input, map selection, saved routes, and GPX import and export.
  • Support for 18 languages, dark and light themes, settings backup and restore, and guided first-open setup.
  • Separate FOSS and Google Play apps that can be installed together. The Google Play version also includes fused location, Android Auto, and optional Google POI search with your own API key.
