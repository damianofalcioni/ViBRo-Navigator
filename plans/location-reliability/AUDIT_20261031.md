### Confirmed slow motion was displayed as zero speed

Independent raw observations could establish MOVING at 0.25–0.35 m/s while the
display resolver still suppressed every speed at or below 0.35 m/s. GPS telemetry
and the trip maximum consequently disagreed with moving time and distance.

The resolver now receives the motion assessment and permits confirmed movement
down to the existing 0.2 m/s timing floor. Unconfirmed low speed and the existing
position/displacement quality gates remain protected. Coverage includes precise
native speed, filtered speedless slow walking, unconfirmed slow drift and full
Route/Straight Line telemetry and trip maximums. Two existing regression methods
failed after adding the missing consumer assertions against unchanged production.

### Stationary sleep could combine different observations or sources

The independent stationary-sleep window retained its origin, cadence and shortened
fallback mode across a nearby fused/GPS handover. It also accepted raw/filtered
pairs with different providers or measurement times when their coordinates were
nearby. The service can select a retained candidate rather than the incoming
callback, so proximity alone is insufficient to establish observation identity.

Sleep eligibility now requires raw/filtered provider and monotonic measurement
time identity. Provider changes reset the full confirmation window, trigger and
cadence before independently spaced sample admission. A clustered source change
cannot leave old evidence intact, and a new source cannot inherit a shortened
fallback window. Five new regressions failed against unchanged production and
pass after correction. Acquisition remains active while evidence is rebuilt.
No alarm, subscription, notification, permission or wake-lock policy was added.
