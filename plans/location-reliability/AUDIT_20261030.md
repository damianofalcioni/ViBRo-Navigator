## Findings and corrections

### Chained beelines advanced beyond unconfirmed required targets

The completion loop verified a stop but did not consume it until after processing
all reachable legs. While that stop was still pending, checks for a different
nearby stop or final destination could pass without confirming that target.
Arrival notifications remained gated, but the next beeline could disappear and
route progress/history advance beyond its unconfirmed target.

Two new regressions failed against unchanged production code: an intermediate
stop followed by another nearby stop, and an intermediate stop followed by a
nearby final beeline. The correction consumes a confirmed stop and collects its
arrival event inside the completion policy before evaluating the next leg. Each
required target consequently starts its own confirmation window. The evaluator
retains responsibility for route/history handoffs. Short return legs can still
complete in the same fix; existing beeline/export regressions remain applicable.
A third regression covers two co-located stops and a co-located final beeline.
No arrival radius, uncertainty limit or confirmation duration was relaxed.

### Battery diagnostics omitted the planned source comparison

The battery log recorded capacity-property percentage without the broadcast
percentage or their difference. It also read the counter and percentage again
when computing the counter status, so validation could describe a different
reading from the raw value in the record. Full status was interpreted differently
from the trip reader when the plugged extra was missing.

Samples now include the percentage source, broadcast percentage and signed
property-minus-broadcast difference. Invalid/missing comparisons remain unknown;
disagreement alone does not invalidate a reading. Raw values and validation use
one read of each counter/capacity property. The logger shares charging/full
interpretation with the existing snapshot reader. Sampling remains callback
driven, at most once per minute, with no new receiver, timer or wake lock.
Regression assertions failed before the correction on APIs 23, 28 and 33.
