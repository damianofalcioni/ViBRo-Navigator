## Additional finding

Routed turn guidance did not consistently check fix freshness. A calculation/
callback delayed beyond the ten-second evidence lifetime could post an initial
turn using expired cached coordinates and speed. In ordinary Route mode, those
coordinates could also initialize the hint cursor past an earlier maneuver.
Ordinary on-route evaluation and route-start/native-beeline handoffs could consume
turn alerts from a delayed observation still admitted by location selection.
Arrival already used current evaluation/application time, so its freshness tests
did not cover these notification paths.

The repair retains route loading and rapid acquisition, withholds the initial
signal for missing/expired/future observations and initializes the hint sequence
without advancing from stale coordinates. Stale planner progress holds pending
alert flags and cannot credit warmup/departure stability. Fresh progress remains
able to produce ordinary due alerts. Route and Round Trip, freshness boundaries,
ordinary alerts, both direct-guidance handoffs and a delayed confirmed speculative
callback are covered by seven new test methods. Six fail against unchanged
production; the seventh protects fresh and boundary-time guidance.
