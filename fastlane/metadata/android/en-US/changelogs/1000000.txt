First stable release of ViBRo Navigator.
- Offline BRouter routes, round trips and straight-line guidance.
- Vibration and optional voice directions, including screen-off navigation.
- Destination search, saved routes, GPX import/export and settings backup.
- 18 languages and dark/light themes.
- Separate FOSS and Google Play apps; Google Play adds fused location, Android Auto and optional Google POI search with your own API key.
