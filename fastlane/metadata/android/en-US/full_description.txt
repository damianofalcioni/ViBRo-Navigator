ViBRo Navigator is a lightweight, battery-efficient, offline-first navigation app built around BRouter.

It provides map-free navigation through vibration patterns, notifications, optional concise speech instructions, and minimal on-screen guidance. Route calculation and turn instruction generation work offline; an internet connection is required only when searching for a destination.

Features:

* Offline routing through BRouter
* Vibration-based and smartband-friendly turn guidance
* Minimal on-screen and optional spoken instructions
* Background and screen-off navigation
* BRouter routing profile selection
* Route, round-trip, and straight-line modes
* Straight-line guidance without BRouter
* Intermediate stops and blocked-road rerouting
* Destination history and coordinate or map-link handling
* OpenStreetMap place and map-based POI search
* Nearby streets extracted from BRouter data
* GPX route and GPS-fix export
* Dynamic GPS acquisition to reduce battery usage

Important notes:

* BRouter must be installed separately for routed turn guidance.
* Straight-line navigation works without BRouter.
* The app does not require Google services.
